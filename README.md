# Alex's Galaxy Math 🚀

An adaptive "explore the galaxy" math game for Alex. Every correct answer adds warp fuel and blasts an asteroid or UFO. Five fuel cells make a warp jump to the next planet or star, and each new place shows a fact card that gets saved to the Space Logbook.

Live: https://howibrettyourmother.github.io/alex-galaxy-math/ (also a tile in [Alex's hub](https://howibrettyourmother.github.io/alex-games/))

- 45 skills across 12 topics, levels 1–24. Level 1 is addition within 10; level 24 is order of operations and logic.
- Adaptive: a correct answer adds +0.3, plus a speed bonus and a streak bonus. A miss subtracts 0.45 and eases back to easier skills. Topics that need practice get extra weight.
- Parent view: hold the ⚙️ gear for 1.5 seconds.
- Blitz mode (90 s) is optional. The normal game has no timers.

## Space facts and sources
Each real fact was checked against NASA or other reputable sources. "Space Legend" planets are made up for fun and carry that label.

- **The Moon** (Lv 1, Real Fact): 12 astronauts walked on the Moon between 1969 and 1972. There is no wind or rain on the Moon, so their footprints can last for millions of years! _Source: NASA_
- **The Sun** (Lv 2, Real Fact): The Sun is a star — the only star in our solar system. About 1.3 million Earths could fit inside the Sun! Sunlight takes about 8 minutes to zoom to Earth. _Source: NASA Sun Facts_
- **Mars** (Lv 3, Real Fact): Mars looks red because its dust has rusty iron in it. Mars has the biggest volcano in the solar system: Olympus Mons, about 3 times taller than Mount Everest! Mars has 2 small moons, Phobos and Deimos. _Source: NASA Mars Facts; NASA/JPL Olympus Mons_
- **Asteroid Belt** (Lv 4, Real Fact): The asteroid belt is a giant ring of space rocks between Mars and Jupiter. The biggest thing in it is Ceres, a dwarf planet. _Source: NASA_
- **Dino Planet** (Lv 5, Space Legend): Legend says T. rexes in space helmets surf on comets here! Their roar is so loud it makes the volcanoes sneeze. _Source: Made up for fun_
- **Jupiter** (Lv 6, Real Fact): Jupiter is the biggest planet. More than 1,300 Earths could fit inside it! A day on Jupiter is only about 10 hours long. Its Great Red Spot is a giant spinning storm, like a huge hurricane. _Source: NASA “What Is Jupiter?”_
- **Io (Jupiter’s moon)** (Lv 8, Real Fact): Io is the most volcanic world in the whole solar system! Its volcanoes are powered by squeezing from Jupiter’s gravity. _Source: NASA Io Facts_
- **Europa (Jupiter’s moon)** (Lv 9, Real Fact): Europa is covered in a shell of ice. Scientists think a giant salty ocean is hiding under the ice! NASA’s Europa Clipper spacecraft launched in 2024 to study it. _Source: NASA Europa Facts; Europa Clipper_
- **Saturn** (Lv 10, Real Fact): Saturn’s rings are made of chunks of ice and rock. Saturn is so light for its size that in a big enough bathtub, it would float! _Source: NASA Saturn; NSSDC fact sheet_
- **Lava World** (Lv 12, Space Legend): Legend says the lava here is so hot it bubbles like soda. Rock monsters play dodgeball with fireballs — bring your shields! _Source: Made up for fun_
- **Pluto** (Lv 13, Real Fact): Pluto is a dwarf planet. In 2015, NASA’s New Horizons spacecraft flew past Pluto and found a giant heart-shaped icy plain! _Source: NASA New Horizons_
- **Kuiper Belt** (Lv 15, Real Fact): The Kuiper Belt is a huge ring of icy worlds past Neptune. In 2019, New Horizons flew past Arrokoth — the farthest object ever explored up close, about 4 billion miles away! _Source: NASA Arrokoth; New Horizons_
- **Proxima Centauri** (Lv 17, Real Fact): Proxima Centauri is the closest star to our Sun. Its light takes a little over 4 years to reach us. It is a small red dwarf star, and at least one planet goes around it. _Source: NASA Hubble; NASA Exoplanets_
- **Orion Nebula** (Lv 19, Real Fact): The Orion Nebula is a giant cloud of gas and dust where new stars are being born — a star nursery! It is about 1,500 light-years away, and you can see it without a telescope in Orion’s sword. _Source: NASA Hubble Messier 42_
- **Betelgeuse** (Lv 21, Real Fact): Betelgeuse is a red supergiant star in Orion’s shoulder. If it sat where our Sun is, it would swallow the orbits of Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars! Someday it will explode as a supernova. _Source: NASA (2024); NASA JPL Night Sky_
- **Sagittarius A*** (Lv 23, Real Fact): Sagittarius A* is the giant black hole at the center of our Milky Way galaxy. It is about 4 million times heavier than our Sun! Scientists showed the first picture of it in 2022, using telescopes all around the Earth. _Source: Event Horizon Telescope; NASA JPL_

Word-problem space numbers (level 11): 6 crewed Apollo landings × 2 moonwalkers = 12; Jupiter day ≈ 10 h; sunlight ≈ 8 min; New Horizons at Pluto 2015 and Arrokoth 2019; Mars has 2 moons; Olympus Mons ≈ 3× Everest.
